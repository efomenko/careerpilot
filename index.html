    Record actual results separately from potential impact, and keep supporting information together with each achievement.

    About verification statuses

    Unverified means the achievement has not been confirmed. Needs confirmation means you marked it for further review. Verified means you deliberately reviewed and confirmed it. Verification is a user decision; evidence and measured metrics do not automatically verify an achievement.
